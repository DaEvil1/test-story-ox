# Metric Interaction Map

Individual criterion scores hide systemic effects: improving X often moves Y.
This map records the known couplings so edits are made with the system in
mind. Three coupling types:

- **Reinforcing** — the metrics rise together; invest in either, both benefit.
- **Tension** — optimizing one erodes the other past a point; must be balanced,
  not maximized.
- **Trios** — three metrics forming a system; the system fails if any corner
  starves.

## Trios

**Dread Triangle** — Antagonist pressure (15) · Tension peaks (41) · Fear lexicon.
Dread requires all three: a refuser, a spike, and body-language of fear.
*Measured:* analyzer joins tension ledger × emotion categories — peak
chapters with ≤2 feeling-categories are running on plot, not dread.

**Payoff Triangle** — Plant/payoff (43) · Fair-play (18) · Reread yield (49).
Plants feed fairness; fairness makes rereads rich. One orphan plant taxes all
three corners.

**Cost Economy** — Pressure consequences (15) · Moral weight (23) · Stakes
escalation (32) · Catharsis (35) · Cost acknowledged (37).
A cost system, not five scores: extraction without acknowledgment reads as
cruelty; acknowledgment without catharsis reads as bookkeeping.

**Breath Pair** — Escalation (5) · Pacing variation (8).
Escalation creates the debt; variation services it. Tightening one demands
loosening the other on purpose.

## Editing rules of thumb derived from the map

1. Any cut over ~100 words → re-check attachment beats in that chapter (Density Squeeze).
2. Any new cost on-page → schedule or confirm a valley within the next chapter (Cost Economy).
3. Any new subversion → cite its seed in surprise_ledger same session (Trust Contract).
4. Any refrain added → check FQ headroom AND whether a character owns it (Voice pair).
5. Any new world term → WT check immediately; terms are load-bearing for integration (27↔45).
