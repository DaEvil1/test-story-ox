# Craft Narrative — Qualitative Improvement Tracker

The rubric measures defect absence. This file tracks **presence of quality**:
specific passages, moments, and structural choices that make the book better
than it was. Updated each cycle. Not scored — described.

---

## What is better now than at the first assessment (r1 → current)

### The obedience sequence replaced the injury arc

**Was:** Keji forced past a refusal; the door took two fingers; she screamed
"high and unfamiliar." Visceral but arbitrary — the punishment didn't match
the crime.

**Now:** She offers custody; the node refuses the mark gently; she waits.
"The room accepted the mark. Gently—not a grant, not a surrender. A hand
saying: I see you seeing me." The cost shifted from flesh to witness-burden,
which is thematically correct: the curator's punishment for forcing is not
pain but the knowledge that she should have known better.

### Sera crossing into the Curators' hall

**Added:** "Tell him the cough is where he still is." Sera walks into the
institution bare-wristed, installs herself on the marble bench, and reads
the private ledger room aloud in a neutral voice to test whether it holds
weight. Then leaves without touching anything.

This gave Sera a scene of pure agency that costs her nothing physically but
everything politically — and it made Ari's later sacrifice legible as
solidarity rather than mere enforcement.

### Madam Oyelaran

**Added:** Named, female, institutional authority. Reads the plaque order
twice ("once as policy, once as a mother"), then strips Ari's seals herself.
One gesture: dual reading, real consequence, no dialogue needed.

### "Remember to eat"

**Added:** One recorded message in an unrecognized language, playing on loop
in the descent walls, "patient as wallpaper." Does nothing for plot. Does
everything for world-inhabitation.

### The child's shoe on the quay

**Added:** The tide leaves it between their feet. Salt-stiff, laces tied.
Neither reaches for it. Next wave takes it back.

Not placed by any character. Not explained. The setting produced a moment
that nobody controls and neither person can resolve. This is what texture
looks like when it's alive rather than decorative.

### "Enough."

**Changed:** "That is enough for now" (five words) → "Enough." (one word).
Same meaning, different weight. Keji's voice compressed to match her arc.

## Pattern across all improvements

Every change falls into one of two categories:

1. **Character autonomy restored** — moments where characters act according
   to their established nature rather than plot convenience
2. **World texture deepened** — details that exist because the world would
   produce them, not because the reader needs them

Neither category is measurable by rubric score. Both are measurable by
reading the book before and after.
