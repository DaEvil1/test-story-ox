# Zero-Based Audit — 2026-08-25 (first run)

Prompt: assume the manuscript is a sophisticated but fundamentally mistaken
solution to its premise. Identify the five most load-bearing creative
decisions; describe the story where each never existed; would any dominate?

## Load-bearing decisions examined

### 1. Ira never appears alive (absence-as-presence)

**The story without it:** Ira is present — confrontation, reconciliation or
final fight, motive spoken aloud. A grief-and-forgiveness drama between two
people, with the Archive as setting.
**Verdict: KEEP.** Absence IS the grief engine (#12's entire mechanism) and
the cadence-in-the-walls device depends on it. The alive version is a more
conventional book that dominates nothing. Residual value logged: an
alive-Ira universe remains viable sequel space.

### 2. The erasure motive stays unknown ("necessary" unresolved)

**Without it:** a revealed reason — catastrophe prevented, a threat only
Ira saw. Closure replaces ache.
**Verdict: KEEP.** Revealing converts the reread engine (#49), the central
ambiguity (#19), and judgment pressure (#50) into exposition. The reader
ledger shows both interpretations live at ch11 — the ambiguity is
*functioning*, which is the strongest evidence against spending it.

### 3. Resolution = administrative amendment (room-making, not exposure)

**Without it:** dramatic public revelation arc — truth shouted, city
convulsed, vindication or ruin.
**Verdict: KEEP.** The amendment is the theme performed (#21/#36's "best
structural feature"). The exposure version is the obvious story this one
exists to refuse.

### 4. The Limit never explained

**Without it:** ontology answered — subsystem, ghost, governor. P2 would be
satisfied; #19/#50 collapse to trivia.
**Verdict: KEEP** — with one honest note: P2's ask has now survived three
rounds, and the frozen reader shows no confusion cost from the ambiguity.
The ask may simply be wrong for this book. Re-examine only if a natural
in-story test ever presents itself; do not manufacture one.

### 5. No human antagonist (opposition is systemic + personal-absent)

**Without it:** a Curator investigator hunts Keji post-discovery — urgency,
a face for pressure, ch5–ch10 gain a chase spine.
**Verdict: ESCALATE-SEED (not dominant).** The strongest divergent finding
of the audit. It would fix real measured gaps (#8 pacing swing, #32 civic
urgency, P6's non-wall action beat). It does not dominate the current story
— it describes a *louder* one — but it is too good to lose.
→ Seed harvested to `drafts/discovery_buffer.md`: *"institutional pressure
made personal: a named Curator investigator who follows the plaque order
backward"* — available for any expansion pass or sequel space.

(Also examined and kept without contest: memory-as-physical-material conceit;
Keji's permanent injury as cost architecture.)

## Outcome

Five KEEP, one seed harvested, zero branches opened. The audit found no
dominating alternative universe — notably, every "story without X" was
*more conventional*, which is the audit working: it tests whether polish has
been sanding away strangeness. It hasn't. The strangeness that remains is
the part three personas call the book's identity.

Next audit due: before content-complete declaration.
