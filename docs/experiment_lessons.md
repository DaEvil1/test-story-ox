## Cycle 1 — 2026-08-25 (deaf-wall, ch5-fork, sera-crosses, ira-alive)

### What was tried
- dive → worldbuilding integration (asking the wall game)
- fork → ch5 opening three cold alternatives (blind-ranked)
- veto ×2 → Sera crosses into institution; Keji obeys annotation refusal

### What worked
- **HARVEST over ACCEPT for seductive mutations.** ira-alive generated the most exciting probe prose but would have changed the book's species. The framework correctly distinguished creative energy from fitness.
- **Fork with blind ranking beat incumbent.** The polished contextualized version lost to a cold alternative — proving sunk-cost advantage is real and surmountable.
- **Character-veto produces consequences, not just justification.** Sera crossing forced downstream reactions (terminal log, Ari's position) rather than being retrofitted.

### What didn't work / surprised us
- **Zero-base audit constructed a false binary** (subtle incumbent vs obvious exposure). The veto branch found a third option neither side imagined. Lesson: verbal audits systematically underrate executable alternatives.
- **Cross-chapter regex bleeding** inflated dialogue share from 16% to 33% because one quote imbalance let matches span boundaries. Fixed by per-file computation. Lesson: always compute text metrics per-unit, never on joined corpora.

### P4 (craft): "The wall-game integration passed my one test — mundane behavior, not announced folklore. But watch it: two data points can become a pattern."
### P3 (emotional): "Sera crossing gave her agency that costs something. More of this."
### P6 (momentum): "ch5 widow-first opening improved entry speed measurably."


## Cycle 2 — 2026-08-25 (ch02-voice, senior-curator, tide-gift)

### What was tried
- dive → one recorded voice in the silent descent (ch2)
- fork → naming and motivating the senior curator (ch11)
- wild → the tide leaving a child's shoe on the quay stones (ch11)

### What worked
- **Single-line inhabitation.** "Remember to eat" does more worldbuilding than a paragraph of description. The lesson from Cycle 1 (mundane > announced) held.
- **Naming as agency.** Giving Madam Oyelaran a name and a dual reading transformed her from enforcement into person without adding dialogue.
- **The world producing moments.** The child's shoe is not placed by any character; it arrives because the setting has moods. This is new territory.

### What surprised us
- C6 scored the texture integration 8.5/10 specifically because it was NOT announced as culture. The best worldbuilding is behavior, not lore.
- The council diverged most on whether Oyelaran's dual reading strengthens or dilutes Ari's sacrifice. Two valid readings coexisting = thematic depth functioning.

- None. Infrastructure freeze holding. All changes were prose-level.
