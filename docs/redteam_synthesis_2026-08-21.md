## Cross-cutting conclusion

history. Nothing needs cutting. Six of eight gaps require *new scenes or
scene-extensions*; two (#M6, partially M5) are line-craft.

## Candidate direction packages

### Package A — "The Curator's Art" (~400–600 words)
One scene showing memory-editing as gift: a Curator (could be Keji herself,
on a routine case — also serves M8) performs an edit that visibly *helps* —
a trauma survivor given back a wedding day with the terror removed, handled
with craft and consent. Directly answers M1, serves M8, feeds #22/#31/#40.
Risk: must not undercut the story's argument; the scene should be genuinely
good *and* leave one hairline wrongness only Keji notices.

### Package B — "Lio, carried" (~150–250 words)
One physical Lio memory: weight asleep against her shoulder, salt-smelling
hair, the specific heaviness of carrying a sleeping child upstairs. Slides
into ch2's coin memory or ch11 quay. Answers M2; amplifies M3's setup.

### Package C — "The break" (~80–150 words)
At the ribbon reveal (ch10), Keji's managed grief cracks once — not sobs;
something smaller and worse (laughing while crying, or winding the ribbon
too hard around her hand). Answers M3; risks nothing structural.

### Package D — "City on a map" (ledger + ~50 words)
Canon geography paragraph + one transit sentence per chapter-endpoint; plus
one ticking element (the plaque has an install date; the council review sits
three days out). Answers M5; cheap.

### Package E — "The long sentence" (line pass, ~10 sentences)
One deliberately long, risked sentence per chapter during the next line pass,
targeting stdev ≥8. Answers M6; pure craft.

### Package F — "Madam Senior Curator" (~100 words)
Gender-flip one institutional figure (the Council speaker in ch11 arrives as
a woman; or Ari's own mentor revealed female). Answers M7 without retconning
anything on-page.

### Not recommended now
- M4 (Ira as fuller presence): both ambiguity readings are balanced at 4
  evidence items each; adding Ira-person risks tipping q1 and unbalancing
  the story's central ambiguity. Defer unless Package B/C naturally carry it.
- Any plot/clock escalation beyond D's soft tick: conflicts with the
  deliberate quietness that three personas praised as the book's identity.
