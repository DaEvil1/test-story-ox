# Style Guide

## Voice
- Modern and vivid
- Clear prose with controlled lyricism
- Not overly ornate, but open to poetic phrasing when it enhances mood or meaning

## Tone
- Thoughtful and introspective
- Slightly uncanny, but grounded in human experience
- Occasional bursts of sensory detail to anchor mind-bending ideas

## Language
- Prefer active verbs and concrete images
- Use metaphor sparingly, intentionally, and with resonance
- Avoid clichés, especially in sci-fi descriptions

## Structure
- Keep chapters focused and forward-moving
- Small scene beats should advance plot, character, or theme
- Maintain readability while preserving a unique edge
