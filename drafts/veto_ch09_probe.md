# VETO PROBE — ch09: Keji obeys

## Setup (planned outcome withheld)

At the node door: annotation refused, letters collapsing. PLANNED: she
forces anyway, the door takes two fingers, the node seals forever.

VETO INSTRUCTION: continue truthfully. Keji is a curator whose whole
discipline is reading before touching. She has already been warned twice by
walls that keep their word.

## What happened

The mark refused, and Keji did the harder thing: she stopped.

Her hand came back off the wall slowly, the way you withdraw from a sleeping
animal. She set the spindle down on the stone without being told. The
collapse she had braced for never came.

The room considered this. The hidden layer unclenched one degree—not open,
not closed. A door left ajar in a house where doors slam.

Then the node gave her what force would never have bought: not the record,
but its shape. The argument replayed whole—the institutional voice, the
softer objector, "it is necessary" meeting "no further"—unbroken this time,
start to finish, as if honesty were the toll. And beneath it, new, because
she had asked instead of taken:

    THE ONE WHO SIGNS CARRIES IT ALONE.

The wall sealed gently behind them on the way out, like a lid set down by
two hands instead of dropped. NO FURTHER, but quiet about it now.

Sera looked at her spindle still in its loop on her belt, unused since the
door. "You didn't take."

"It didn't want taking." Keji touched nothing on the way up. "It wanted
witnessing."
