# FORK PROBE — ch5 opening, three cold versions

Converge point for all variants: "There are two ways to use this," Sera said.

## VARIANT A — incumbent (wall-first)

The wall in the Drift was not the same wall that had resisted her. It had
become a scrape of raw memory, exposed where the city had not yet covered
it. Keji leaned against it and felt the ink pulse under her palm.

Sera watched her. "What did you bring back?"

Keji put the recorder on the wall between them. The red light blinked. The
first line played in her ears again, precise and thin: AUTHORIZE RESET.
PRESERVE NETWORK. REMOVE CHANNEL.

Sera's fingers tapped the console. "So this is the thing. The door is a
brick wall. The Limit is a hand on the wall."

## VARIANT B — widow-first

Down in the Drift, a woman sat against a breathing wall with an echo-bottle
open in her cupped hands. Her husband's last hours played raw around her:
half sentences, a cough, somebody's radio. She had refused the Curators'
offer to clean it. "The cough is where he still is," she said — to no one,
to everyone.

Sera brought Keji to her before anything else.

"This is what keeping looks like," Sera said quietly. Then she nodded at
the recorder in Keji's hand. "Now show me what you carried out of there."

Keji put the recorder on the wall between them. The red light blinked. The
first line played: AUTHORIZE RESET. PRESERVE NETWORK. REMOVE CHANNEL.

## VARIANT C — dialogue-cold

"Do you think the child is Lio?"

Sera asked it before Keji had finished sitting down on the Drift steps, and
the recorder sat between them with its red light blinking like something
alive.

"The name is there," Keji said. "The hand is there. The voice is there."

Sera's fingers tapped the wall. "So this is the thing. The door is a brick
wall. The Limit is a hand on the wall."
