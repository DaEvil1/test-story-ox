## What happened

They walked back through the Drift in silence, and at the edge where the
Drift becomes the market, Sera stopped.

"I need to go away for a while."

Keji waited. Sera didn't elaborate immediately, which was unlike her.

"That man back there. I've seen it before — the bursts. They're getting
closer to the surface." She looked at the maintenance key on her belt, the
one she'd carried for years without using until Keji arrived. "I know every
unsealed wall in this district. If one more goes while I'm standing here
with a Curator's key on my belt..."

"You think you caused it?"

"I think I stopped preventing it."

Sera unclipped the maintenance key from her belt and held it out. Not to
give — to show. Then she put it back.

"Three weeks. Maybe four. I need to walk until the walls stop talking to me."
She turned toward the market. "Don't call me unless the building is falling
down."
