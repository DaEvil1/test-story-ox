# Protagonist: Keji Lian

- Name: Keji Lian
- Role in story: Protagonist and memory researcher
- Core belief: Memory can be healed and improved through deliberate editing.
- Contradiction: Keji is haunted by a memory error that feels more alive than the versions she keeps.
 - Arc goal: Learn whether the Archive's stability is worth the cost of losing part of herself — and whether restoring certain erased truths is worth the personal and civic fallout.
 - Key relationships:
   - Mentor: Ari, an archivist who thinks order is safety.
   - Catalyst: Sera, a Drift member who lives in unpredictability.
   - Ira Nash: a former partner whose choices are central to Keji's hidden past.
   - Lio: a younger sibling (or close charge) whose disappearance was suppressed by an erased event.
- Why they matter to the theme: Keji sits between the desire to make sense of life and the fear of erasing its messier truths.
- Voice & style notes: Thoughtful and precise, with flashes of lyrical observation when she experiences memory glitches. When Keji thinks of Ira or Lio, the prose should tighten and grow raw, revealing buried emotion and the weight of avoided decisions.
