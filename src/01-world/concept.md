# Core Concept

A mind-bending science fiction story built around the idea that memory is a programmable medium and reality is a layered construct.

The protagonist lives inside a city-sized archive where each individual stores fragments of their life as mutable data. The archive is a sentient infrastructure, and its patterns influence the way people perceive their own identities.

At the center of the story is a conflict between two worldviews:
- One that believes memory should be curated and optimized to achieve clarity and stability.
- Another that insists memory must remain wild, paradoxical, and uncensored because human meaning emerges from its chaos.

This tension unfolds against a backdrop of shifting cityscapes, improbable technologies, and a subtle philosophical current questioning whether any self is more than a sequence of edited traces.
