# Canon & Continuity Ledger

Single source of truth for established facts. `tests/manual/consistency.md`
checks chapters against this file. When a draft establishes new canon, record
it here in the same session; if a draft contradicts this file, either fix the
draft or amend the ledger with a `docs/decisions.log` entry.

## Characters

- **Keji Lian** — protagonist; memory editor in the Archive's editing hall.
  Carries: the glitch strip (Ira's voice, taken in ch1), the coin (found ch2),
  a recorder with captured fragments (ch4+). Avoids engaging Ira's memory
  directly; reacts physically when it surfaces.
- **Ari** — senior curator, Keji's mentor. Guards sealed layers; hands Keji a
  heavily redacted packet in ch5; knows of and tolerates her private ledger
  (ch8); witnesses and accepts the amendment (ch11). Hinted to have touched
  the sealed record himself once (ch3).
- **Sera** — Drift member, catalyst. Guides Keji to hidden layers; carries a
  maintenance key for wall panels (ch9). Philosophy: let memory show its
  shape, don't force it.
- **Ira Nash** — Keji's former partner; signed the erasure authorization
  (revealed ch3, heard ch4). Never appears in person; recurs as voice/cadence
  and the word "necessary". Whether Ira was right is never resolved.
- **Lio** — child connected to Keji (younger sibling or close charge);
  victim of the erased event. Identified by: looped-J handwriting, a crude
  lighthouse drawing, the coin, and the child's line "if I keep the light,
  then the dark can go away."
- **The Limit** — boundary force in the Archive; not confirmed to be a person.
  Manifests as refusal ("NO FURTHER"), pressure, resetting walls, sealed
  doors; sometimes speaks in Ira's cadence. Never explained or defeated.
- **The Archive** — city-sized living archive built around the ocean;
  quasi-character; balances order and entropy; its preservation is also
  omission.

## Established rules & facts

- Memory is physical material: threads, ribbons, strips; walls carry readable
  script; a hidden layer sits beneath public varnish.
- The erasure directive: "AUTHORIZE RESET. PRESERVE NETWORK. REMOVE CHANNEL."
  Ira Nash signed; the child was "assigned to a private ledger." The reason
  is never shown — this is deliberate.
- The Limit enforces boundaries physically (doors refuse, walls reset) and
  verbally ("NO FURTHER", "KEEP PRIVATE").
- The hidden layer's final position (ch6, ch10): keep the node private; let
  the public surface carry only what it must.
- The amendment (ch11): "guardianship record misfiled" correction; memorial
  plaque in the market; private ledger retained for authorized curators.
