# Setting

## The Archive

The Archive is a living archive-city built around one of the last natural oceans on Earth. It is both computer and organism, a place where architecture behaves like software and memory behaves like material.

- Buildings are made of adaptive lattices that shift according to stored narratives.
- Streets can reroute themselves to reflect collective moods.
- The ocean is a data reservoir, its tides carrying packets of remembered weather, music, and code.

## Factions

- The Curators: People who work to organize and refine memory streams, believing that a stable archive produces a stable self.
- The Drift: A loose network of those who embrace the Archive's emergent noise, letting memories mutate and mix.

## Technology

- Echoes: Small devices that extract, replay, and alter personal memory.
- Palimpsest Layers: Architectural strata where old stories are overlaid on new ones, making the city itself a living narrative.
- Somatic Threads: Interfaces that let people feel the Archive's pulse through touch and movement.
