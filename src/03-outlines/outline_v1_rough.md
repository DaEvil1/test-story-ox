# Rough Outline v1

## Act 1
- Introduce Keji and the Archive. Show memory editing as a common practice.
- Keji observes a glitch in her own archive layer that she cannot explain.
- Meet Ari and Sera, establishing the two philosophies.
- Keji decides to investigate the anomaly.

## Act 2
- Keji explores hidden strata of the Archive.
- She discovers that the glitch is connected to an old erased event.
- Sera offers a different way to access the memory: through the Drift.
- Conflict deepens as Ari warns that the old event could destabilize the Archive.

## Act 3
- Keji confronts the erased memory and the Archive itself.
- She must choose between restoring continuity and preserving chaos.
- Climax: The Archive reveals that the city’s current order is built on a collective forgetting.
- Resolution: Keji accepts an imperfect self and decides how she will carry the Archive forward.
