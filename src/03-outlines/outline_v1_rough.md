# Rough Outline v1

## Act 1
- Introduce Keji and the Archive. Show memory editing as a common practice.
- Keji observes a glitch in her own archive layer that she cannot explain.
- In the background, subtle anomalies appear: impossible footsteps, a vanished message, a phrase that seems to belong to no one.
- Meet Ari and Sera, establishing the two philosophies.
- Keji decides to investigate the anomaly and senses there is something else shaping the Archive.

## Act 2
- Keji explores hidden strata of the Archive.
- She discovers that the glitch is connected to an old erased event.
- Hints appear of a peripheral presence called The Limit: a phantom antagonist whose influence is felt in the city’s architecture and in other characters’ strange recollections.
- Sera offers a different way to access the memory: through the Drift.
- Conflict deepens as Ari warns that the old event could destabilize the Archive, and Keji begins to wonder whether The Limit is a ghost, a system bug, or a philosophy made flesh.

## Act 3
- Keji confronts the erased memory and the Archive itself.
- She must choose between restoring continuity and preserving chaos.
- Climax: Keji encounters The Limit directly, a figure or force that represents the opposite of her final choice and forces her to question whether reality is a story being told to her.
- Resolution: Keji accepts an imperfect self and decides how she will carry the Archive forward, leaving The Limit’s reality ambiguous and perhaps unresolved.
