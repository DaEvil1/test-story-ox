# Detailed Outline v2

## Story Overview

Keji Lian is a memory specialist in the Archive, a city built around a living reservoir of remembered experience. Her work is to make memory tidy, but a persistent glitch leads her toward a hidden event and a peripheral force called The Limit. As the Archive itself seems to whisper, Keji must decide whether a self made of edited traces is still human.

## Act 1: The Fault Line

1. Keji is introduced at work in the Archive's editing room. The prose shows memory as tactile: threads pulled, inked fragments reconfigured, breath held in data pulses.
2. A glitch appears in Keji’s own archive: a line of text she never wrote, a corridor that will not stop rearranging, and an echo on her palm from a voice that is not hers.
3. In the market of living walls, Keji finds a vanished message painted in a language no one claims; the shape of the letters resembles a childish scrawl Keji remembers from Lio.
4. At the Curators' hall, Ari explains the Archive's purpose and warns that deep layers are sealed for a reason. His care is clear, but so is his fear.
5. Keji meets Sera in the Drift, where living memory spills into the streets. Sera argues that some fragments must be allowed to run wild. She mentions a hidden boundary called The Limit, a line the city refuses to cross.
6. Keji decides to investigate the anomaly, stepping into a world of memory that resists being neatly categorized.
