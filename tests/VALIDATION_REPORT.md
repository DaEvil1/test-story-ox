1. **Plot Question 2:** "Why was the old memory erased?" is hinted but not fully explored. Middle chapters must answer this through Keji's investigation and discovery of Ira's choice.

2. **The Limit's direct presence:** Chapter 1 hints; final chapter shows it lingering. Middle chapters should bring Keji into more direct encounter with The Limit (whether as voice, rule, or presence).

3. **Ari's full arc:** Introduced in Chapter 1; shown accepting in final chapter. Middle chapters should deepen his struggle and show why he made the choices he did.

4. **Sera's role:** Present in Chapter 1 and final chapter. Middle chapters should show whether she helps or pushes Keji, and what her own philosophy costs.

5. **Lio's memory:** Chapter 1 hints (scrawl, coin). Middle chapters should bring Lio alive through recovered fragments before the final choice to preserve them.

6. **Keji's descent:** Final chapter jumps to the decision. Middle chapters must show Keji's journey into hidden strata and her growing understanding of what was sacrificed.
