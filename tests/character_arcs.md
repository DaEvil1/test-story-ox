# Tests: Character Arcs

- Keji should show a clear tension between editing memory and honoring messy truth.
- Ari should be sympathetic without being a villain.
- Sera should feel like a plausible counterpoint, not a caricature.
- The Archive should feel alive and have agency in the story.
- The antagonist should feel present without being fully accounted for, and their influence should make the reader question the boundaries of reality.

## Validation Notes
- Check whether Keji's inner conflict appears in chapter scenes.
- Confirm Ari's choices are motivated by care and fear.
- Confirm Sera's chaos is rooted in a coherent philosophy.
- Confirm the antagonist is introduced subtly and becomes direct in the climax.
