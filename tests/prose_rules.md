# Tests: Prose Rules Compliance

This test validates adherence to our hard prose principles (see docs/style_guide.md).

## Rules to Check

1. **No explanations of character choices or internal states:**
   - ❌ "Keji felt uncertain about her decision."
   - ✓ Show her hands trembling, her hesitation in action.

2. **Show through action, dialogue, behavior—never through authorial commentary:**
   - ❌ "She was afraid of the Archive's response."
   - ✓ She held her breath. The terrace shuddered.

3. **No meta-commentary about the story or journey:**
   - ❌ "Keji learned that memory was more than facts."
   - ✓ Let the story teach this; no narrator summarizing.

4. **No laying out arguments or conclusions through narrator voice:**
   - ❌ "She chose a middle path that honored both truth and survival."
   - ✓ Show her writing the amendment, facing Ari, walking away.

5. **Avoid "moral of the story" moments; end on sensory immediacy:**
   - ❌ "And so Keji learned to carry edges where necessary."
   - ✓ The coin in her pocket. Her stiff legs as she rose. The walk back to market.

6. **Trust the reader; they will reach conclusions:**
   - ❌ Multiple paragraphs explaining why Keji chose this path.
   - ✓ Brief scene showing the choice; let readers interpret.

## Validation

For each chapter:
- Scan for lines that explain character internal state; rewrite to show through action.
- Scan for narrator commentary about themes or lessons; remove or convert to dialogue/action.
- Check final paragraphs don't summarize character growth or conclude the story thematically.
- Verify all conflicts are enacted through dialogue/behavior, not introspection.
