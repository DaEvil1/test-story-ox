# Tests: Motif Usage

Validates that recurring motifs (water/tides, palimpsest, The Limit) are woven throughout and serve the story thematically.

## Motifs to Track

**Water & Tides:**
- Should appear when memory is flowing, shifting, or overwhelming.
- Should carry sensory weight (brine, current, rhythm).
- Should not be purely decorative; each use should anchor an abstract memory concept.

**Palimpsest:**
- Should describe surfaces—walls, the city itself—as layered and readable.
- Old stories should show through new ones.
- Should convey the idea that nothing is ever fully erased, just covered.

**The Limit:**
- Should appear as a boundary, a voice, a rule, or an absence.
- Should sometimes echo Ira's cadence.
- Should remain ambiguous: is it a person, a system, a metaphor, or something else?

## Validation Checklist

- [ ] Water/tides imagery appears at least 3-4 times across the full story, always anchoring memory shifts.
- [ ] Palimpsest language appears at least 2-3 times, describing the city and memory layers.
- [ ] The Limit is named or felt in at least 2-3 distinct scenes, with varying levels of clarity.
- [ ] At least one scene where The Limit's voice sounds like Ira's (or Ira's voice echoes The Limit's logic).
- [ ] No motif appears so often it becomes clichéd or predictable.
- [ ] Each motif usage advances theme, not just atmosphere.
