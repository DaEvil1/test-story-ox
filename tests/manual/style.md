# Tests: Style & Prose Quality

## Core Style Principles

- The prose should feel modern and precise.
- Look for opportunities to use poetic language where it enhances mood.
- Maintain a readable pace: no long digressions unless they serve the theme.
- Check for recurring motifs like memory, water, and city rhythm.

## Prose Adherence (Critical)

The mechanical subset is enforced by `python tools/check_story.py` (rules in
`tests/automated/rules.yaml`); the full principles are in
`tests/automated/prose_rules.md`. Key reminders for manual review:

- No explaining character internal states; show through action.
- No authorial commentary on themes or lessons.
- No narrator voice guiding reader interpretation.
- Chapter endings should land on sensory immediacy, not thematic summary.

## Voice Consistency

- Keji's voice should be precise and controlled, with flashes of lyricism when she's close to memory or emotion.
- Archive descriptions should feel both mechanical and alive (it's a system but also a quasi-character).
- When The Limit's voice appears, prose can tighten or fragment.
- Dialogue should be sparse and carry weight; it should reveal character, not explain.

## Rhythm

- Sentences should vary in length. Short sentences for tension or revelation; longer for atmosphere or memory flow.
- Paragraphs should build toward small moments of clarity or confusion.
- Avoid purple prose; poetic language should serve the story, not decorate it.
